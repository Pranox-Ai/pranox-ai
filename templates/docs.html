🚀 Getting Started

Pranox AI runs entirely in your browser — there's nothing to install.

  1. Open the Dashboard
  2. Sign in with Google to unlock Email Writer and Resume Builder (AI Chat and Deep Research work without signing in)
  3. Pick a tool and describe what you need in plain English
  4. Generate, copy, or download your result in seconds

✉️ Email Writer

Go to /email, describe your topic, and choose a tone — Professional, Friendly, or Formal. Pranox AI writes a complete email you can copy with one click.

Tip: the more context you give ("follow-up after a final-round interview for a backend role"), the more specific the result.

📄 Resume Builder

Go to /resume and fill in your name, target role, skills, experience, and education. Pranox AI structures it into a clean Summary / Skills / Experience / Education format, ready to copy or download as a PDF.

💬 AI Chat Assistant

Go to /chat for an open-ended assistant — ask questions, brainstorm, debug code, or generate images, all in one conversation thread.

🔎 Deep Research

Go to /deepsearch when you need an answer synthesized from multiple sources rather than a single response — useful for comparisons, summaries, and broader topics.

🔐 Account & Privacy

Sign-in uses Google OAuth — we never see or store your Google password. See our Privacy Policy for full details on what we collect and how it's used.
