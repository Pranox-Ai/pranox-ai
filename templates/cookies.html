Cookie Policy

Effective Date: January 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Pranox AI uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit or use our platform.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. They help websites remember information about your visit, such as your login state and preferences.

2. Cookies We Use

Pranox AI uses a minimal set of cookies, strictly limited to what's needed to run the service:

• Session Cookies: Keep you signed in after Google login so you don't have to re-authenticate on every page
• Security Cookies: Help protect your session and prevent unauthorized access
• Analytics Cookies: Basic, aggregated usage data (via Google Analytics) to understand how the product is used and improve it

3. What We Don't Do

We do NOT use cookies to:

• Sell your data to advertisers
• Track you across unrelated third-party websites
• Build advertising profiles

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by services we rely on, including:

• Google OAuth — for secure sign-in
• Google Analytics — for anonymized usage statistics

These providers have their own cookie and privacy practices, which apply alongside this policy.

5. Managing Cookies

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. If you disable session cookies, you will not be able to stay signed in, and features like Email Writer and Resume Builder (which require login) will be unavailable.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as the platform evolves. Continued use of Pranox AI after changes means you accept the revised policy.
